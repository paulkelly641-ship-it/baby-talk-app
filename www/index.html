📦 Publish Kit — Apple Upload

📱 No Mac? Just your phone
Steps 1–4 below can all be done in Safari on your phone. Only the final build & upload needs a Mac — and Codemagic does that in the cloud from your GitHub repo, so you still never need your own Mac.
  1. Create the app in App Store Connect (Safari on your phone): My Apps → + → New App. Name: Baby Talk, Bundle ID: com.babytalk.app, SKU: anything (e.g. babytalk001).
  2. Paste the store listing below (name, subtitle, description, keywords). Upload the 1024×1024 icon (store/app-icon-1024.png) and screenshots (6.9″ and 6.5″ iPhone).
  3. Create the 3 In-App Purchases: Monthly $9.99 and Yearly $59.99 as auto-renewable subscriptions in group “Baby Talk Premium”; Lifetime $99.99 as a non-consumable (not a subscription). Product IDs are in the table below.
  4. Add bank & tax info in App Store Connect → Agreements, Tax, and Banking (needed before you can be paid).
  5. Push this project to GitHub (new repo, e.g. baby-talk-app), connect it in Codemagic, and run the ios-workflow. Codemagic builds the IPA on a cloud Mac and uploads it to App Store Connect. Then pick the build in App Store Connect and Submit for Review.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Baby Talk (“the app”) is made for babies and toddlers, ages 0–3, and their parents.

What we collect: nothing. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that receives your data.

On-device only: your child’s first name (if you enter one), progress stars, and favorites are saved only on your own device, in the app’s local storage. They never leave the device.

No ads, no tracking. The app shows no advertisements, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and contains no links to outside websites.

Purchases: subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. Purchase status is checked on-device through RevenueCat to unlock the app.

Children’s privacy: the app is designed for use by a parent together with their child. A parental gate (a simple question only a grown-up answers) sits in front of all purchases and settings.

Contact: questions? Email paulkelly641@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Subscriptions: Baby Talk offers auto-renewable subscriptions (Monthly $9.99, Yearly $59.99) and a one-time Lifetime purchase ($99.99). Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.

Auto-renewal: subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Lifetime: the Lifetime purchase is a one-time payment. It does not renew and does not expire.

Use: Baby Talk is a learning aid, not medical or speech-therapy advice. If you have concerns about your child’s speech development, talk to your pediatrician.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple’s Report a Problem page.
