## Description (4000 characters max)
My Baby Smarter helps babies and toddlers learn to talk — one tap at a time.

Big, colorful cards speak every sound, word, and sentence out loud. Just tap
and listen, then say it together!

THREE STAGES IN ONE APP
• Babble & Sounds (6–12 months) — ba-ba, ma-ma, da-da, and animal sounds
  like moo, baa, woof, and meow.
• First Words (1–2 years) — mama, dada, ball, dog, milk, more, up, bye-bye
  and more everyday words.
• Little Sentences (2–3 years) — "I want milk", "More please", "Big dog",
  "I love you", and other first sentences.

• "Now you say it!" practice card after every tap
• Progress stars to celebrate every try
• Favorites — save the words your baby loves most
• Parent tips from real-life talking practice
• A grown-up answers a quick question before any purchase

SAFE FOR LITTLE ONES
No ads. No tracking. No links out. Everything works offline, and nothing
about your child ever leaves the device.

SUBSCRIPTION
Monthly $9.99, Yearly $59.99, or Lifetime $99.99 (one-time). Payment is
charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions
renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the
current period. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings > Apple ID >
Subscriptions.
