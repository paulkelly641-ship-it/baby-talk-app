Privacy Policy — Baby Talk

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Baby Talk (“the app”) is a speech-learning app made for babies and toddlers, ages 0–3, and their parents.

What we collect: nothing

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that receives your data.

On-device only

Your child’s first name (if you enter one), progress stars, and favorites are saved only on your own device, in the app’s local storage. They never leave the device, and deleting the app deletes them.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no advertisements, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and contains no links to outside websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. Purchase status is checked on-device through RevenueCat to unlock the app. RevenueCat receives only the anonymous purchase receipt data needed to verify the subscription — no name, email, or child information.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for use by a parent together with their child. A parental gate (a simple question only a grown-up answers) sits in front of all purchases and settings. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and the “Last updated” date above will change.
